---
name: compare-theories
description: Compare two or more theories, models or methods on clear criteria
when_to_use: "X vs Y", "difference between", "which is better", "karşılaştır", "farkı nedir"
---
1. Define each item from a source (one sentence each, cited).
2. Choose 4-6 criteria fitting the question (assumptions, domain of validity, predictions, experimental support,
   computational cost, open problems).
3. For each item × criterion find a sourced statement; where they make different numeric predictions, compute
   both with `python` for the same inputs.
4. Present a comparison table, then where they agree (limits where one reduces to the other), where they differ
   and which experiments distinguish them.
5. Don't declare a winner unless the sources do; say in which regime each one applies.
